About the founder

Practical help from someone who understands field work and paperwork.

Courtney understands how quickly the office side can get heavy when the owner is focused on billable work. She has handled the real back-office load behind a trade business: client records, accounts receivable, invoice discrepancies, permits, vendors, local officials, timesheets, continuing-education records, and qualification paperwork.

Her paralegal background adds careful document handling, confidentiality, legal research habits, proofreading, case-file organization, court-document assembly, and deadline discipline. LightBack is designed to be the bridge between doing everything alone and hiring full-time office staff — real help from someone who respects blue-collar work because she has been close to it.

  • Writing, proofreading, correspondence, spreadsheets, CRM, Google Workspace, DocuSign, Clio, LexisNexis, and Westlaw
